ABOUT TENSORTONIC

TensorTonic is an ML learning platform built around implementation. Instead of
watching lectures, you read short theory and then write the code that makes
the concept real, from gradient descent to attention to LoRA.

The platform is for engineers learning ML hands-on, candidates preparing for
ML interviews, and self-taught practitioners who want their understanding to
come from building rather than from passive reading.
WHAT TENSORTONIC OFFERS

- Problems: coding problems across classical ML, deep learning, NLP, computer
  vision, reinforcement learning, and GPU kernels in CUDA and Triton.
- Study Plans: ordered learning tracks that walk you through a domain end to
  end, such as Cracking Deep Learning and Foundations of PyTorch.
- Research Papers: pick a paper and implement its core idea from scratch with
  the original text alongside.
- Projects: build a full ML system from scratch, end to end. Current projects
  include fine-tuning an LLM with LoRA, building a RAG agent, and chatting
  with a PDF.
- ML Math: linear algebra, probability, calculus, and optimization for ML,
  taught by implementing the math.
- Interview Prep: role-targeted tracks for ML Engineer, Data Scientist, and
  adjacent roles.

ABOUT TENSORTONIC
=================

TensorTonic is an ML learning platform built around implementation. Instead of
watching lectures, you read short theory and then write the code that makes the
concept real, from gradient descent to attention to LoRA.

The platform is for engineers learning ML hands-on, candidates preparing for ML
interviews, and self-taught practitioners who want their understanding to come
from building rather than from passive reading.


WHAT TENSORTONIC OFFERS
=======================

- Problems: coding problems across classical ML, deep learning, NLP, computer
  vision, reinforcement learning, and GPU kernels in CUDA and Triton.
- Study Plans: ordered learning tracks that walk you through a domain end to
  end, such as Cracking Deep Learning and Foundations of PyTorch.
- Research Papers: pick a paper and implement its core idea from scratch with
  the original text alongside.
- Projects: build a full ML system from scratch, end to end. Current projects
  include building a RAG agent, a tokenizer, and a small autograd engine.


FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
==========================

Q: What kinds of math topics does TensorTonic teach?
A: Linear algebra, probability, calculus, and optimization, all framed in the
   context of ML and taught by implementing the math.

Q: What domains do the coding problems cover?
A: Classical ML, deep learning, NLP, computer vision, reinforcement learning,
   and GPU kernels written in CUDA and Triton.

Q: Do I just watch videos?
A: No. Every concept is learned by writing code. You read a short theory section
   and then implement the idea yourself, with tests that check your work.
